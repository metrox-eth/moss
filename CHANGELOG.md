# Changelog

Hardware revisions. Dates are when it was real, not when it was planned.

- **V0.6** (October 2026, prototype being assembled): modular compute and perception plates (Jetson or Raspberry Pi; RealSense or Orbbec Gemini), XT30 power block, 12 V to 5 V converter for the Pi, hull R7, tracks 70 teeth at 10 mm pitch.
- **V0.5** (1 October 2026): redrawn on the real V0.3 robot; one-piece hull, two tensioners per track, mounts for every board, magnets for the bin. Release candidate for the open source CAD. 107 printed parts.
- **V0.4** (24 September 2026, drawn, parts printed): revised hull, screw-terminal controller, one dual motor driver.
- **V0.3** (22 September 2026): first prototype. First drive on 22 September, arm and gripper on 26 September.
