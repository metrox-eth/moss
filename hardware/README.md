# Hardware

Design in Fusion 360. The first prototype (V0.1) is being assembled from the first prints; whatever is odd or not optimal is corrected in the CAD as it is found, without reprinting. The corrected design, V0.3, is the one that will be published here: STL/STEP for every printed part, plus the fastener list (type, thread, length, head, quantity, and what goes into PETG with heat-set inserts). Until then, the photos in `media/` and the list in `docs/bom.md` are the reference.
