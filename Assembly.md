**Power wiring:** the complete rear charging connection must carry **5 A continuous**. The main switch carries the rover load (**15 A design requirement**), not just the charger current. See the [charging path and switch requirements](hardware/power/custom-3s4p-battery/#rear-charging-port-and-cable).
